tfhe-rs's published p-fail for the set is 2^-129.58 (noise formulas incl. FFT error; the
oracle's `tfhe_noise` prints pre-BR σ 4.80). The sampling interval of σ at these counts spans
several units of log2 p-fail, so the point estimates (between about 2^-124 and 2^-130) do not
resolve p-fail at 2^-128. The pooled σ of the three pipelines lie inside each other's 95 % CIs.
The RNS row's higher σ (+5.5 % variance over exact64) cannot come from its arithmetic: the whole
PBS-output term (×5) is about 2 % of the pre-blind-rotation variance, and the RNS PBS output is
less noisy than tfhe-rs's (section 1). The argument for the engine's p-fail is structural, in
docs/BUDGET.md §4: KS and MS are bit-identical to tfhe-rs, and the PBS output variance is below
tfhe-rs's, whose formulas give 2^-129.58.
